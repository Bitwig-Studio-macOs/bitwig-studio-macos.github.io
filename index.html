Bitwig Studio 6.0.5

Bitwig Studio is a highly customizable DAW for music creation and performance. Realize your ideas across every stage of production with a workflow tailored to you.

From sound design to full compositions, take command of your process. Record, arrange, improvise, or perform—seamlessly. Modular devices, unified modulation, and deep integration put you in control.

Key Advantages

Custom Workflow

Choose display profiles, layer edits, and histogram editing. Streamline from idea to track with bounce-in-place and automatic slicing.

Modular Sound Design

Build with container devices, built-in instruments, effects, and VSTs. Modulate any parameter via macros and modulators.

Expressive Performance

Audio and note expressions, smart controller integration, and Open Controller Scripting API. Perform with precision and spontaneity.

Unified Control

Access every aspect of production. Combine recording, arrangement, and live performance in one environment built by musicians.

Stable and Refined

Version 6.0.5 fixes Keyfilter+ note expressions and macOS crashes. Reliable power for your creative flow.

How It Works

1. Set Your Stage
Launch Bitwig and select a display profile. Import audio, notes, or VSTs to start building.

2. Design and Modulate
Use container devices for sounds. Map modulators to parameters for dynamic control.

3. Edit and Arrange
Layer edits, apply expressions, slice automatically. Bounce in place to refine quickly.

4. Perform and Export
Integrate controllers, improvise live. Finalize tracks with full workflow access.
